— Our Story

A Legacy of Fire, Craft & Quiet Luxury

Founded by Chef Élodie Marchand, Maison Noir blends classic French technique with New York energy — every plate a study in restraint and flavour.

From our dry-aging room to the charcoal hearth, each course is built around three ingredients at peak season. Our cellar holds over 400 labels, and our dining room seats just 48 guests a night — intimacy is the ultimate luxury.
