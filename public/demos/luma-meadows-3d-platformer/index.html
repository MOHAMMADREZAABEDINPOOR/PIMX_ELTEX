Pip the fox-cub's Sun Shards were scattered across the floating meadows by the grumpy Grumblets! Run, bounce and stomp your way through honey-hills, wobbly bridges and lava-bubble caves to raise the Sun Flag 🚩

How to play 🗺️

🦊 Move with A/D or arrows. Hold a direction to build speed — Pip leans, wobbles and squashes like jelly!

🌀 Jump with SPACE. You get coyote-time (late jumps still work!) and jump-buffering. With a Cloud-Feather 🪶 you can double-jump!

👾 Enemies (Grumblets, Spikers, Hoppers): jump on their heads to stomp them! Touching them from the side costs 1 ❤️. A Sun-Seed ☀️ shield lets you bonk them safely.

🪙 Coins / Sun Shards: collect all 40 for a perfect rank. Chests (press E) hide big bundles.

🚩 Checkpoints save your respawn. Fall in a pit or lose all hearts and you restart there (−1 life).

⚠️ Traps: spike-bushes, lava-bubbles, swinging moon-pendulums and flame-flowers. Watch the rhythm!

🏁 Goal: reach the giant Sun Flag! Time + coins = your rank (C → S+).
